Frequently Asked Questions

1. Why is my keyword trigger not firing?

Double check that your connected Instagram account is set as a **Creator** or **Business** account (personal accounts do not support API automation). Additionally, check your **Integration Security** on the Overview tab to make sure webhooks are active.

2. My comments are captured but DMs are delayed?

This is expected and healthy! DMOrbit utilizes a dynamic **Meta Safe-Pacing Engine** (staggering DMs by 5–15 seconds). This keeps your account completely safe from automated spam flags. You can monitor active delays in the pacing queue widget on your Overview screen.

3. What does "Token Expired" mean?

Meta security tokens expire every 60 days or when passwords are changed. To resolve this, simply go to your **Instagram Account** page and click **Refresh Token** to safely reconnect via official OAuth. We never ask for or store your Instagram password.
