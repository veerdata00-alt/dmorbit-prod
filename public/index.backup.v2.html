Frequently Asked Questions

Does DMOrbit use official Meta APIs?
Yes. We strictly use the official Instagram Graph API provided by Meta. Your account remains 100% safe and compliant with Instagram's terms of service.
Can I automate story replies?
Absolutely. You can set up campaigns that trigger specific DMs whenever a follower replies to your active Instagram Stories.
Can I connect multiple Instagram accounts?
Yes, our Creator and Pro plans allow you to connect and manage multiple Instagram accounts from a single DMOrbit dashboard.
How do DM limits work?
Each plan comes with a monthly allowance of automated DMs. Depending on your plan, unused DMs may even roll over to the next month so you never lose what you paid for.
Is DMOrbit safe?
Yes, our smart pacing engine limits the speed of outgoing messages to simulate human behavior, ensuring your account is never flagged for spam.
