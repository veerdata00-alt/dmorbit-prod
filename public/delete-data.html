Data Deletion Instructions

Required for Facebook / Meta App Compliance

According to Facebook Platform rules, we have to provide a User Data Deletion Callback URL or Data Deletion Instructions URL. If you want to delete your activities and data for the DMOrbit App, you can remove your activities by the following instructions:

  1. Go to Your Facebook Account's "Settings & Privacy". Click "Settings".
  2. Go to "Security and Login".
  3. Look for "Apps and Websites" and you will see all of your apps activities.
  4. Select the option box of "DMOrbit".
  5. Click the "Remove" button.

Complete Account Deletion

If you wish to completely wipe your DMOrbit account, associated email, and all saved automation rules from our database, please log into your DMOrbit Dashboard, navigate to Settings, and click "Delete Account", or email us directly at support@dmorbit.app.
