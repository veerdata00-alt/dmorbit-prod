Data Deletion Instructions

At DMOrbit, we value your privacy and provide a simple way to delete your data and revoke our access to your Instagram account.

How to Delete Your Data

1 Disconnect via Dashboard: Log in to your DMOrbit dashboard and navigate to the "Settings" or "Integrations" section. Click "Disconnect Instagram" to remove our access tokens.
2 Remove via Facebook:
  • Go to your Facebook Profile's "Settings & Privacy".
  • Click on "Settings".
  • Look for "Apps and Websites" in the left sidebar.
  • Find "DMOrbit" and click "Remove".
3 Request Full Account Deletion: If you wish to permanently delete your DMOrbit account and all associated logs, please send an email to support@dmorbit.app with the subject "Data Deletion Request". We will process your request within 48 hours and send you a confirmation email.

What Data is Deleted?

Upon receiving a deletion request, we permanently remove:

  • Your Meta Access Tokens.
  • Your Automation history and logs.
  • Your user profile information (Email, Name, Profile Picture).

Note: Once deleted, this information cannot be recovered.
