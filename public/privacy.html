Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 07, 2026

Welcome to DMOrbit ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our Instagram automation platform.

1. Information We Collect

When you use DMOrbit, we collect certain information to provide our services:

  • Authentication Data: We use Firebase and Meta OAuth to securely authenticate you. We do not store your passwords.
  • Meta Platform Data: With your permission, we access your Instagram Business Account ID, Facebook Page ID, and access tokens to automate responses to comments and messages.
  • Usage Data: We store logs of automated responses (keyword triggered) to provide you with dashboard analytics.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data solely for the following purposes:

  • To provide the Instagram Comment-to-DM automation service.
  • To track the performance of your automations.
  • To ensure compliance with Meta's Platform Policies.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your access tokens are stored securely in our encrypted database. We use industry-standard security measures to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, or destruction of your data.

4. Data Deletion

You can disconnect your Instagram account at any time via the DMOrbit dashboard. To request full deletion of your account and associated data, please follow the instructions on our Data Deletion Instructions page.

5. Third-Party Services

DMOrbit integrates with Meta (Facebook/Instagram) and Firebase. Your use of these services is also governed by their respective privacy policies.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@dmorbit.app.
