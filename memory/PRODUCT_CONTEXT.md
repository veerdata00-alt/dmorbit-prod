# Product Context

**Ideal Customer:**
Creators and small businesses leveraging Instagram for audience growth and lead generation.

**Target Market:**
Instagram users seeking to automate engagement (DMs, comment replies, story replies) without violating Meta's terms of service.

**Current Positioning:**
A simple, compliance-first Instagram automation tool that focuses on ease of use ("Launch a new campaign in under 2 minutes") rather than complex bot-building workflows.

**Competitor Landscape:**
- ManyChat
- Chatfuel
- Other complex chatbot builders

**Differentiation:**
- Extreme simplicity: focused exclusively on Instagram.
- Built specifically for humans to understand, rather than visual node-based visual programming.

**Pricing Strategy:**
- Freemium model with upgrade tiers (Free, Pro).

**Long-Term Vision:**
Turn Instagram engagement into a reliable, automated CRM funnel.

---

### Fact/Assumption Split
- **Facts:** Built on official Meta API. Has Free and Pro plans. Integrates a frontend dashboard with a Node backend.
- **Assumptions:** Users prefer simplified campaign creation over complex node editors.
- **Unknowns:** Exact conversion rates from Free to Pro, exact competitor feature parity in future sprints.
